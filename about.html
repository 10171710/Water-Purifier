About AquaPure

Pure Water Is Our Promise

For 16+ years we've helped families and businesses drink safer, better-tasting water — with honest pricing and care that lasts.

Our Mission

Make safe, great-tasting drinking water accessible and affordable to every household and business — and keep every purifier running at peak performance for life.

Our Vision

A city where no family has to wonder about the quality of their drinking water — starting with 100,000 protected homes by 2030.

Our Values

Transparency in pricing, authenticity in parts, punctuality in service and genuine care for every customer who opens their door to us.

Our Journey

16 Years of Clean Water

2009

A Single Van & a Vision

Founder Arjun Mehta starts repairing purifiers from a Maruti Omni, serving 3 neighbourhoods in the west zone.

2013

First Showroom & Sales

AquaPure opens its first experience centre and begins selling RO and UV purifiers with free installation.

2017

AMC & 10 Centers

Launch of the Annual Maintenance Contract programme and expansion to 10 service centers across the metro.

2021

Digital Service Platform

Online booking, digital certificates, live technician tracking and a customer dashboard for every AMC holder.

2026

25,000 Families Strong

48 centers, 120 certified technicians and a 98.7% retention rate. And we're just getting started.
