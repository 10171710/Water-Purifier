Installation Process

Installed, Tested & Running in 24 Hours

Order before 2 PM and our certified technicians will have your purifier installed and water certified the same evening.

Step 1

Book Your Slot

Choose a date and 4-hour time window online, by phone, or at any experience centre. Instant confirmation via SMS + email.

Step 2

Pre-Installation Call

Our technician calls to confirm your water source (borewell/municipal/tanker), kitchen setup and any special mount requirements.

Step 3

Free Water Test

On arrival, we test TDS, pH and hardness of your input water to fine-tune the purifier settings for your source.

Step 4

Clean, Neat Installation

Wall mount or tabletop as preferred, with tidy plumbing, a drain line for RO, and full leak testing. Zero drilling mess in rented flats.

Step 5

Demo & Quality Check

We run the purifier, taste-test the water with you, check output TDS, and walk you through filter change indicators and maintenance basics.

Step 6

Digital Certificate

You receive an installation certificate, warranty card and AMC offer — all accessible in your customer dashboard anytime.
